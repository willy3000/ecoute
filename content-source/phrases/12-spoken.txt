## Tu becomes t'
9 | T'as faim ? | You hungry? | 1 | Spoken form of "Tu as faim ?" (also "Est-ce que tu as faim ?"). Dropping the u of "tu" before a vowel is not used in standard writing. | | informal | question-044
10 | T'es où ? | Where are you? | 1 | Spoken form of "Tu es où ?", itself a relaxed form of "Où es-tu ?". | | informal | question-013
11 | T'as raison. | You're right. | 1 | Spoken form of "Tu as raison". | | informal | everyday-032
12 | T'as vu ? | Did you see that? | 2 | Spoken form of "Tu as vu ?". | | informal
13 | T'inquiète ! | Don't worry! | 2 | Very informal short form of "Ne t'inquiète pas". | | informal | feeling-018
## Il y a becomes y a

15 | Y a pas de problème. | There's no problem. | 1 | Spoken form of "Il n'y a pas de problème". Both "il" and "ne" disappear. | | informal | everyday-042
16 | Y a quoi à manger ? | What is there to eat? | 2 | Spoken form of "Qu'est-ce qu'il y a à manger ?". | | informal | spoken-047
## Il faut becomes faut

## Question word at the end

19 | C'est quoi ? | What's that? | 1 | Spoken form of "Qu'est-ce que c'est ?". | | informal | question-007
20 | Tu veux quoi ? | What do you want? | 1 | Spoken form of "Qu'est-ce que tu veux ?". It can sound abrupt. | | informal | question-008

## Fillers and connectors
32 | Euh, je sais plus. | Um, I can't remember. | 2 | "Euh" is the French "um", a hesitation sound. "Ne" is dropped. Standard writing: "Je ne sais plus". | | informal
33 | Du coup, on fait quoi ? | So, what do we do? | 2 | "Du coup" is a very common spoken way of saying "so". | | informal
34 | En fait, c'est facile. | Actually, it's easy. | 1 | In "en fait", the t is usually pronounced. Fine with anyone.
35 | Bon, ben, à demain. | Okay then, see you tomorrow. | 2 | "Bon, ben" signals the end of a conversation. | | informal
36 | Allez, on y va ! | Come on, let's go! | 1 | "Allez" here is a call to action, not a real "you go". | | informal
37 | Quoi de neuf ? | What's new? | 1 | A friendly informal greeting. The f of "neuf" is pronounced. | | informal
38 | Rien de spécial. | Nothing special. | 1 | The usual answer to "Quoi de neuf ?". Fine with anyone.
39 | Désolé, je savais pas. | Sorry, I didn't know. | 2 | "Ne" is dropped. Standard writing: "Désolé, je ne savais pas". | | informal
## Short exchanges
40 | Ça va ? Ouais, et toi ? | You okay? Yeah, you? | 1 | A typical exchange of two lines between friends. | | informal
41 | On y va ? Ouais, on y va. | Shall we go? Yeah, let's go. | 1 | A typical exchange. "On y va" is one of the most useful phrases in spoken French. | | informal
42 | Merci ! Pas de quoi. | Thanks! Don't mention it. | 1 | "Pas de quoi" is short for "Il n'y a pas de quoi". | | informal
43 | Tu viens ? J'arrive ! | You coming? On my way! | 1 | A typical exchange. "J'arrive" can be said before you have even moved. | | informal
