## Telling the time
1 | Quelle heure est-il ? | What time is it? | 1 | "Heure" and "est" run together. The t of "est-il" is pronounced: "é-til". | Il est quelle heure ?
2 | Il est une heure. | It's one o'clock. | 1
3 | Il est huit heures et demie. | It's half past eight. | 1 | The t of "huit" links to "heures".
4 | Il est dix heures et quart. | It's quarter past ten. | 1 | The x of "dix" sounds like a z before "heures".
5 | Il est midi. | It's noon. | 1
6 | Il est minuit. | It's midnight. | 1
7 | Il est trois heures moins le quart. | It's quarter to three. | 2 | Liaison in "trois heures": "troi-z-heures".
